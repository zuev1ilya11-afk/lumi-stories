# Дом чёрных роз — visual canon and continuity

Approved source: `approved-character-bible.jpeg` (user supplied 2026-10-03).

All originals were generated with built-in Imagegen, individually opened and visually reviewed before WebP integration. The first Evelyn and Adrian images lock their faces; subsequent shots use those images as identity references. First Evelyn uses the identical Evelyn face in a Victorian updo and ivory lace gown, and appears only inside a painted portrait. Adrian's historic and modern face is identical. No Lucian or Isabelle appears.

## Production assets

Thirty individually authored shots are in `public/assets/house-of-black-roses/v1`. The manifest records per-file prompts, SHA-256 hashes, byte counts and acceptance notes. The initial master shots follow the user brief and canon; derivative/edit prompts are retained verbatim in the manifest.

- Six environments: exterior, main hall, stairs, bedroom, midnight corridor and gallery.
- Evelyn: neutral, arrival, curious, concerned, frightened, determined, shocked; one extra determined night variant without coat.
- Adrian: neutral, formal, guarded, warning, soft, tense, final.
- Event CG: doorway greeting, black roses, midnight handle, petal trail, historic dress/hands, historic face, exact nameplate, double portrait and final two-person reaction.
- Exterior also provides the first Ravenhall reveal and story cover.

Images use the full composition and are optimized to 768×1152 WebP. Encoding is the only deterministic image operation: no crop, anatomy hiding, retouch or darkening was used to repair defects.

## QA findings resolved through Imagegen

- Midnight lever original exposed an open door gap. Regenerated closure: flush shut door, no exposed bolt.
- Bedroom and concerned Evelyn background originally included red roses. Corrected to black roses without modifying face/body.
- Petal trail originally had burgundy petal interiors. Corrected individual petals and bud to jet black.
- Added blouse-only determined Evelyn for scenes after removing her coat. Earlier close-ups retain coat. All post-bedroom beats use the appropriate wardrobe.

## Anatomy and identity acceptance

Full-frame review covered shoulder/elbow/wrist chains, hands and finger overlap, facial proportions, gaze, clothing, props and perspective. The doorway hand rests naturally against the open door, with no complex grip. The portrait has two people and four natural hands; no hand is placed on another person's shoulder. Its detail insert retains natural overlapping hands. Final two-person scene has distinct natural limbs, matching contemporary clothes and faces.

Portrait progression: dress/hands → historic face → Evelyn reaction → Cyrillic brass plaque → recognition → full double portrait → Adrian/final reaction. Neither the full double portrait nor Adrian's painted face is used before its reveal. Detail framing is a narrative reveal, not a defect concealment. Exact dates are legible: ЭВЕЛИН РЕЙВЕН / 1879–1901.

Cinematic camera motions remain subtle. Reduced-motion mode uses still frames and instant text. No jump scare, flashing effect or scary sound was added.
